An introduction to the QGIS interface, finding and loading vector data from public repositories, and basic cartography, with additional reading on map design.

This module was taught as part of the Purdue-NSF GIS Cybertraining for the Social Sciences Workshop, a two-day workshop held at Purdue University in 2026 (Day 1, Session A QGIS track, part 1). See the [Workshop 2026 page](https://spatialturn.github.io/workshop2026.html) for the full program.

It is part of [Enabling the Geospatial Turn in the Social Sciences](https://spatialturn.github.io/), a set of training modules developed at Purdue University with support from the National Science Foundation.
